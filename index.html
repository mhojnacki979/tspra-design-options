Homepage design options

Texas Senior Pro Rodeo Association, tspra.com rebuild

Six directions for the new tspra.com homepage, in two groups. Every one puts the next rodeo and the Rodeo Secretary's phone number first, uses big type you can read at arm's length, and works on a phone.

About these drafts

The pages are shown as of June 11, 2026, in the middle of the season, so rodeos 1 to 12 are struck through and Hamilton (rodeos 13 and 14) is next.

The schedule, standings and the Rodeo Secretary's number are real 2026 figures from tspra.com and the results site, used here as sample data. "[Arena name]" marks the Caldwell, Groesbeck and Taylor arenas, which the current site does not name.

Options A to C leave labeled spaces for real TSPRA member photos. Options D to F use free stock action photos as stand-ins, marked on the page; the finished site will use member photos: older competitors in action, faces visible.

Buttons and links in the drafts are for show; only the phone numbers dial.
