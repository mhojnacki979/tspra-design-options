How to enter

Call the Rodeo Secretary on the Monday before the rodeo, between 6 and 9 PM. Have your member number and events ready.

Need to draw out? Call at least 24 hours before the rodeo to avoid a fine.

Match entry fees are paid in cash at check-in.
